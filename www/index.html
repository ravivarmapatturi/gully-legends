How to play

Batting: you see where each ball pitches and where every fielder stands. Tap a zone on the field map and choose a ground or lofted shot. Green zones are good bets, red ones are risky. Balls naturally go to certain zones (a short ball outside off to point, a full ball to the straight field), and gaps beat fielders.

Bowling: set your field, then pick a line and length. Each cell shows how dangerous that ball is against this batter with your current field: bowl to your field. Accurate bowlers land it where you aim; a missed yorker becomes a full toss.

Squad: players earn XP and you earn coins and training points. Spend training points on attributes and coins on trait tokens. Win the league to move up a division.
