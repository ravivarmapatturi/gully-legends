    How to play

    You bowl first: pick a bowler each over, then a delivery each ball. Read the scouting line to find the batter's weakness, and don't bowl the same ball again and again.

    Then you bat: tap the left, middle or right of the screen as the ball reaches the yellow ring. Hit with the line of the ball, not against it.

    Runs, fours and sixes earn hit coins. Spend them on training to give players trait tokens.
